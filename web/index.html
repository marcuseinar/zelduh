A randomly generated top‑down adventure, in the shape of the Game Boy Zeldas.

Play together

Playing on your own.

Up to four players share one world, and one of them can take over a dungeon boss and hunt the others. Everyone in a room is connected directly to everyone else: the relay only introduces you, and the game itself never passes through a server.

Nobody sends a position or a health bar. Every player runs the same simulation and only the buttons travel, which is why a whole world costs a few bytes a frame — and why the game checks, every couple of seconds, that all of you still agree about it.

Using the built‑in art.

The art that ships with the game is original. Nothing from any commercial cartridge is included or downloaded.

If you own a Game Boy game you can drop its ROM here and the engine will read tiles out of your own file to draw the world with. Nothing is uploaded: the file is read in this tab and never leaves your device.
